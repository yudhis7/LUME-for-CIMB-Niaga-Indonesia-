**Lume for CIMB Niaga 1.1.0, the CIMB Niaga edition** of the desktop apps for Windows and macOS.

New in this version:
- **Sign-in screen**, co-branded CIMB Niaga × Lume, with pilot accounts for each team (password `lume2026`). Each person gets their own team's view; `whale.admin` can switch between teams.
- **Keep me signed in** for 7 days, sign-out from the account menu, and a 30-second pause after 5 wrong passwords.
- **CIMB Niaga logo** in the top bar and on the advocate phone app, and a **co-branded app icon**.
- **Fix:** resetting demo data while the advocate app is open no longer leaves an empty strip.

The pilot sign-in is checked on the computer only. In production, Lume uses CIMB Niaga single sign-on, set up with IT & InfoSec.

Downloads:
- **Windows 10/11:** `Lume-for-CIMB-Niaga.exe` (single file) or `Lume-for-CIMB-Niaga-Windows.zip`
- **macOS 14 Sonoma or later:** `Lume-for-CIMB-Niaga-macOS.zip` (Apple Silicon and Intel)

The apps are not code-signed yet. On first launch: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on a Mac go to *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*.
